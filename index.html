My Design Philosophy

Think Big Picture and Back Your Decisions. Too often than not, it’s easy to falter to decision paralysis or not having the confidence to make the call on a solution. Regardless of what is decided, I always assure myself, my clients, and my peers that solutions should be an iterative process. While solutions are an iterative process, making sure that your decisions are grounded by evidence - even it’s a hypothesis.

Similar to the Classic Design Thinking Model: Double Diamond Design Process

This is the main method I refer to for my hybrid occupation of a Product Designer and Product Owner. I learned about this method during my undergraduate years at Portland State University. This type of design thinking helped me expand and narrow down design solutions in every design space I’ve been involved in: Graphic Design, UI/UX Design, Interior Design, figuring out a place to eat… you name it!
