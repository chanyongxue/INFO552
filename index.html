Juanita Vasquez is a Bay Area illustrator whose vibrant work draws from her Latinx heritage and love of bold color. Her prints and illustrations feature botanicals, community scenes, and cultural celebration rendered in a lush, expressive style.

Vendor Resources
Beginner Vendor Tips
  • Start small — pick 1–2 events to test your setup
  • Research the event audience before applying
  • Bring more product than you think you'll need
  • Talk to other vendors at your first event
Pricing Advice
  • Factor in materials, time, and booth fees
  • Research comparable products at similar events
  • Offer a range: entry-level and premium pieces
  • Bring exact change and a card reader
Booth Setup Guides
  • Use height variation to create visual interest
  • Put your most eye-catching work at eye level
  • Have your business cards / IG handle visible
  • Practice setting up at home before the event
Event Preparation
  • Confirm your setup time and load-in details
  • Pack a vendor kit: tape, zip ties, scissors, markers
  • Download your event's floor plan in advance
  • Eat before — there's rarely time during the event
